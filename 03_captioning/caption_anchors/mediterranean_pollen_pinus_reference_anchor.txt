These pollen grains are large, heteropolar monads about 60–90 µm wide including their two air sacs. Light microscopy shows a central, lens-shaped corpus flanked by thin, smooth sacci, so the grain has a distinctive dumb-bell outline in polar view. The corpus exine is tectate and finely verrucate-to-gemmate, giving it a subtle roughness, whereas the sacci remain psilate and very thin.
