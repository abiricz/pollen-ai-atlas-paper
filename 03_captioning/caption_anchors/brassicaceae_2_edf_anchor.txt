These pollen grains are medium-sized, isopolar monads 24–32 µm long and prolate to subprolate. Light microscopy shows three long colpi that taper toward the poles and extend nearly the full grain length. The exine is thin, <4µm They are coarsely eureticulate, meaning the muri of the reticulum walls are connected at the heads so the stout muri surround the rounded lumina over the entire surface. Lumina are < 1 µm wide. In polar orientation the outline is circular, while slight drying can make the colpi sink and produce a weakly lobate profile.
