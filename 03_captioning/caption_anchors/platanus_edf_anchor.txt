These pollen grains are isopolar, spheroidal monads 18–25 µm in diameter. Light microscopy shows three long colpi running nearly pole-to-pole; the colpus membranes are minutely ornamented, giving a clear tricolpate aperture system. The exine is finally reticulate, In polar view the outline is circular, and a subtle three-lobed symmetry reflects the colpi terminations.
