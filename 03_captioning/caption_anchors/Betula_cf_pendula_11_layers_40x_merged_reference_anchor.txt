These pollen grains are small, isopolar monads 25–33 µm in diameter (some smaller shrublike species 22-28 µm) and spheroidal. Light microscopy shows three equatorial circular pores, each bordered by a faint annulus and seated on a low oncus. The exine is thin, tectate and psilate, so the wall appears uniformly smooth. In polar orientation the outline is circular with the three pores forming a regular trefoil pattern.
