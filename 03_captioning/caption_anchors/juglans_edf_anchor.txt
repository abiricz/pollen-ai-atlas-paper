These pollen grains are medium-sized, oblate-spheroidal, isopolar monads 33-54 µm in diameter. Light microscopy shows a pantoporate aperture system 7-11 pores in the equatorial region and 1-6 pores in only one of the polar fields and rarely one further pore in the other polar field; each pore bears a clear annulus and the pore membrane appears minutely ornamented. The exine is thin and looks finely verrucate-scabrate.
