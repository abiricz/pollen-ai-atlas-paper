These pollen grains are small to medium, isopolar monads 16–28 µm in diameter and spheroidal when hydrated. Light microscopy shows three short colpi sets 120 degrees apart; each colpus ends in a circular operculate pore, giving the grain a tricolporate, brevicolporate aperture system. The sexine is thick, tectate and densely echinate, bearing robust spines about 1–2 µm long that rise from a finely perforate background. In polar view the outline is slightly three-lobed, whereas in equatorial view the grain approaches circular.
