These pollen grains are medium-sized, isopolar monads 30-45 µm (some species in this type are as small as 25 µm and some as big as 49 µm) and broadly spheroidal. Light microscopy shows three long colpi, giving a clear tricolpate system; the colpus membranes are minutely ornamented. The exine is thin and shows striate ornamentation, appearing as parallel to slightly wavy ridges across the surface. In polar orientation the outline is circular.
