These pollen grains are small to medium, isopolar monads 14–34 µm in diameter and spheroidal. Light microscopy shows three long equatorial colpi bordered by a narrow margo, yielding a tricolpate aperture system. The exine is thin and distinctly reticulate, with low muri enclosing polygonal lumina. A typical feature of this type is that the lumina gets smaller towards the colpi. In polar orientation the outline is circular.
