These pollen grains are medium-sized, heteropolar monads 30-40 µm in diameter and oblate spheroidal. Under light microscopy they show a stephanoaperturate pattern: four to six circular pores arranged in an equatorial ring; each pore often has a faint annulus. The exine is finely verrucate–scabrate giving a subtle roughness. In polar view the outline is circular.
