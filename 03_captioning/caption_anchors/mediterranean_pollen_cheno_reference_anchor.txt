These pollen grains are isopolar spheres 17-35 µm in diameter. Light microscopy reveals a pantoporate aperture system with 25 to 70 pores, each rimmed by a faint annulus and capped by a delicate operculum. The exine tectate and densely verrucate-to-scabrate with small gemmae, giving the wall a coarse granular texture.
