These pollen grains are small, isopolar spheres 19–27 µm in diameter. Light microscopy shows a pantoporate aperture system: typically 6 to 17 circular pores scattered over the surface; each pore is rimmed by a faint annulus and some species are capped by a thin operculum, with the aperture membranes minutely ornamented. The exine tectate and finely verrucate-to-gemmate, giving a subtle warty texture.
