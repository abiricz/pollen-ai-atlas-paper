These pollen grains are isopolar, spheroidal monads 18–36 µm in diameter. Under light microscopy three (rarely four) long equatorial colpi are visible, each with a small rounded pore, yielding a tri- to tetracolporate aperture system. The exine is thin and distinctly reticulate. In polar orientation the outline is circular, and slight refocusing brings the reticulum into crisp relief.
