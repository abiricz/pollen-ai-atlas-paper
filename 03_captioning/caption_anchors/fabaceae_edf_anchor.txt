These pollen grains are isopolar, spheroidal monads 23–30 µm in diameter. Light microscopy shows three long equatorial colpi, each with a narrow internal pore, giving a tricolporate aperture system. The exine is thin, tectate and finely verrucate-to-scabrate with scattered gemmae, so the surface appears slightly rough. In polar view the outline is circular, and slight drying can render the colpi terminations faintly trilobate.
