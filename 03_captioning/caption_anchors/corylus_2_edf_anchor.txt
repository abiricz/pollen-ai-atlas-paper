These pollen grains are isopolar, spheroidal monads 28–40 µm in diameter. Light microscopy shows three evenly spaced circular pores, each bordered by a delicate annulus and no vestibule atop a large oncus. The exine is thin, tectate and appears psilate to very faintly scabrate, so the wall looks mostly smooth. In polar orientation the outline has a rounded triangular appearance due to the protruding pores.
