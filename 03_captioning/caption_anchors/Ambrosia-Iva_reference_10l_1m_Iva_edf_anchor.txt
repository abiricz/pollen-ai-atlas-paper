These pollen grains are small to medium, isopolar monads 18–24 µm in diameter. Light microscopy shows three short colpi and circular operculate pore, giving the grain a tricolporate, brevicolporate aperture system. The sexine is thick, tectate and densely echinate, bearing robust spines about 1–2 µm long that rise from a finely perforate background. In polar view the outline is slightly three-lobed, whereas in equatorial view the grain approaches circular.
