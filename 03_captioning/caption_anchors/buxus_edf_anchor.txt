These pollen grains are medium-sized, isopolar spheres 29-42 µm in diameter. Light microscopy reveals a pantoporate aperture pattern: more than six circular pores are irregularly scattered over the surface, each pore bordered by a faint annulus; no colpi occurs. The exine is thin, tectate and distinctly reticulate, so a fine network of low muri encloses small lumina across the wall.
