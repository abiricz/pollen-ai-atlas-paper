These pollen grains are medium-sized, isopolar monads 29–50 µm in diameter and distinctly oblate-spheroidal. Under light microscopy they show three brevicolporate apertures: very short colpi broaden into rounded pores, each underlain by a large dome-shaped bulge shaped by a thickening of the endexine; the aperture membranes are finely ornamented. The exine is thin and finely reticulate—low muri forming a delicate net that may appear nearly smooth at 20x. In polar view the outline is circular while distinctly flattened in equatorial view.
