These pollen grains are small, isopolar monads 21–29 µm in diameter and broadly spheroidal in hydrated view. Light microscopy shows three long equatorial colpi, each with a small rounded pore at the equator, yielding a straightforward tricolporate aperture system. The exine is thin and finely finely reticulate to microreticulate, at least at the end of the intercolpium, so the wall appears subtly roughened rather than smooth. In polar orientation the outline is circular; with slight drying the colpi deepen and the amb becomes weakly triangular.
