These pollen grains are very large, heteropolar monads whose full width—including the air sacs—exceeds 100 µm. Under light microscopy each grain shows a central, elliptic corpus flanked by two thin, smooth sacci that make the grain strongly oblate in polar view. The distal face of the corpus carries a single leptoma aperture; no additional pores or colpi occur. The corpus exine is tectate and finely verrucate-to-gemmate, so the wall appears subtly roughened, whereas the sacci remain smooth and thin. In polar orientation the outline is elliptic, with the two air bladders clearly visible.
