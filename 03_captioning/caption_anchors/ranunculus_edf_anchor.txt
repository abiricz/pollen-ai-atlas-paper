These pollen grains are small to medium isopolar monads 14–50 µm in diameter and slightly prolate-spheroidal. The exine is thick and finely echinate, producing a subtle matte roughness. The main distinguishing feature is that each sculptural element stands above a single columella. Light microscopy shows three long colpi with ornamented aperture membranes, giving a tricolpate aperture system rather than a multiporate one. In polar view the outline is circular to weakly triangular.
