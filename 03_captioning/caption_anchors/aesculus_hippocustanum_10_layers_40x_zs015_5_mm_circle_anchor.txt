These pollen grains are small to medium-sized, isopolar monads 22-32 µm and broadly spheroidal. Light microscopy shows three long colpi, each ending in a small rounded pore, yielding a tricolporate aperture system; the aperture membranes are minutely but clearly ornamented. The exine is thin and rugulate to striate, appearing as faint, parallel to slightly sinuous ridges across the surface. In polar orientation the outline is circular.
