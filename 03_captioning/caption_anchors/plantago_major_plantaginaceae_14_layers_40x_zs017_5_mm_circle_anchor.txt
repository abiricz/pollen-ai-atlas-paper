These pollen grains are small, isopolar spheres 21–34 µm in diameter. Light microscopy shows a pantoporate aperture system: typically 5 to 8 circular pores scattered over the surface; each has an unevenly formed pore, with the aperture membranes minutely ornamented. The exine tectate and finely verrucate-to-gemmate, giving a subtle warty texture.
