These pollen grains are large, heteropolar monads about 78–107 µm wide including the air sacs. Under light microscopy each grain displays a central, lens-shaped corpus flanked by two thin, smooth sacci that give the grain a characteristic dumb-bell outline. The distal face of the corpus bears a single leptoma that may appear as a faint linear thinning. Both corpus and sacci are psilate and the exine is very thin except at the leptoma margin. In polar view the outline is elliptic with the two bladders clearly visible.
