These pollen grains are small, isopolar monads 17–23 µm in diameter and essentially spheroidal. Light microscopy shows a heteroaperturate pattern: three long equatorial colpi alternate with three shorter, slit-like pseudocolpi, making the tricolpate grain appear hexacolpate to stephanocolpate, with ornamented aperture membranes. The exine is thin and finely micro-reticulate to punctate, giving a slight roughness rather than a smooth sheen. In polar view the outline is circular and the alternating colpi/pseudocolpi form a faint six-rayed star.
