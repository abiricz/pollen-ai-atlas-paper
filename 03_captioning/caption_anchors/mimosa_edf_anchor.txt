These pollen units are compact polyads composed of sixteen angular monads that tessellate into a regular block approximately 40–50 µm across. Under light microscopy no individual pores or colpi are discernible, so the polyad is effectively inaperturate. Each monad has a thin exine. With careful surface focus a very low verrucate–scabrate to gemmate relief gives a faint granular sheen. Shallow seam lines mark the junctions between monads, and the proximal face is slightly flattened, imparting a subtly heteropolar aspect.
