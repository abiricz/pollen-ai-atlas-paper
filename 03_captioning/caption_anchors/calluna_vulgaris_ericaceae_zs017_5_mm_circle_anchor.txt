These pollen units are tightly packed tetrads: four fused monads form a pyramid measuring mostly <60 µm but some species up to 75 µm. Under light microscopy each monad is tricolpate (some tetracolpate or tricolpate). The colpi of one monad always lines up with the colpi of the adjacent monads. The exine is scabrate, verrucate or psilate. In polar orientation the tetrad outline is almost round.
