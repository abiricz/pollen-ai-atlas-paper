These pollen units are planar tetrads: four fused monads form a roughly square to decussate plate measuring about 41–58 µm across. Under light microscopy each monad shows a single distal pore without a distinct margin. The exine is thin and reticulate, with low muri enclosing large irregular lumina. In polar orientation the tetrad outline is usually rounded quadratic but the individual pollen can also be arranged in a line.
