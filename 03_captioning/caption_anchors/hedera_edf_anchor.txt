These pollen grains are isopolar, spheroidal monads 32–42 µm in diameter. Light microscopy shows three long equatorial colpi bordered by a delicate margo with an elongated pore yielding a clear tricolporate aperture system. The exine is thin, tectate and distinctly reticulate with lumina of distinct difference in size and with well-defined muri enclosing the polygonal lumina.
