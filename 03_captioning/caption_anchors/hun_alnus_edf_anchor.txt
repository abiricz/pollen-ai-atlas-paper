These pollen grains are 19-23 µm in diameter, oblate-spheroidal, and psilate. Under light microscopy they show four to five and sometimes six elongated pores arranged in an equatorial ring; each pore is bordered by a faint annulus and overlies a low oncus. The wall appears smooth at 20x to 40x. In polar view the outline is circular while it is flattened in equatorial view. A special characteristic for this type is that neighbouring pori are connected by arcs or bands of nexinous thickening.
