These pollen grains are medium-sized, heteropolar monads 26–35 µm across with either a round to oval shape or a flattened spindle-shape. Light microscopy shows a single distal sulcus or long colpus-like aperture, giving a monosulcate to monocolpate appearance that differs from annulate monoporate pollen. The exine is thin, tectate and psilate.
